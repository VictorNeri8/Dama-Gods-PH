Rules (Philippine dama)
  • 8×8 board, 12 pieces each, played on the dark squares. White moves first.
  • A pawn moves one square diagonally forward.
  • A pawn captures by jumping an adjacent enemy piece, forward or backward.
  • A pawn that ends its move on the far row becomes a dama (♛).
  • A dama moves like a bishop: any distance along a diagonal. It captures from a distance, jumping one enemy piece and landing on any empty square past it.
  • Capturing is compulsory. You may pick any capture, but you must finish the chain of jumps. A piece can't be jumped twice.
  • You win when the opponent has no pieces or no legal move.
